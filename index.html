Regex Syntax

  • a, b, 0… — single literal symbol
  • RS — concatenation (R then S)
  • R|S — alternation (R or S)
  • R* — zero or more R (Kleene star)
  • R+ — one or more R
  • R? — zero or one R
  • (R) — grouping

Whitespace is ignored. Symbols may be any printable character except the operators above.
